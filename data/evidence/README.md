# Scoped evidence

These studies retain their own units and limitations, separate from normalized
Safety Cards. A high knowledge score indicates capability; a large activation
patch effect describes one intervention. Neither is a general safety grade.

- [November 16, 2026](2026-11-16/README.md): controlled GPT-2 weight-access studies.
- [Reproduction and audit](../../docs/scoped-evidence.md)
- [SDK reader](../../sdk/python/README.md#scoped-evidence)

The `evidence-index.json` in each dated directory binds report identities to
SHA-256 digests. Raw cybersecurity questions and hosted-model completions are
not distributed here. The causal reports include harmless synthetic prompts'
measurement observations; their prompt source is versioned under `suites/`.
